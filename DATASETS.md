# Datasets

Every dataset of the course is either bundled with a standard Python library or generated inside the notebook with a fixed random seed, so no download or upload is needed.

| Dataset | Source | Used on | Licence or origin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Battery pack plant | Simulated in the notebooks from the model of Day 1 | Days 1 to 5 | Created for the course |
| Sensor telemetry | Generated from the plant with noise, bias, drift, quantisation and dropout | Days 1, 2, 3 and 5 | Created for the course |
| Charging depot and fleet | Discrete-event and agent-based simulations with fixed seeds | Day 2 | Created for the course |
| Fault scenarios and trip histories | Simulated with injected faults and ageing | Days 4 and 5 | Created for the course |
