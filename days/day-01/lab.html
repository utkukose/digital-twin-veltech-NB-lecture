Maturity lab

Part A runs the pack and a virtual counterpart in the browser, with the maturity level and the sensor of your choice. Part B classifies six systems as models, shadows or twins. Part C assigns responsibilities to the parts of the reference architecture.

Part A: The maturity bench

Configure the virtual counterpart of the pack and the sensor that feeds it, then press Run and add to scoreboard. The plot shows the true temperature of the pack and what the counterpart believes. The twin may switch the fan; the model and the shadow only watch. Try a sensor bias of minus five degrees with the twin and compare the last two readouts.
